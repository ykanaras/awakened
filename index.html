ABOUT DIMITRI KANARAS

A practice shaped by a decade of study and conscious living.

Dimitri has spent more than a decade studying Buddhism and living a vegan lifestyle rooted in compassion and intentionality. Awakened grew from a desire to share practices that have shaped his own life in a way that feels accessible, sincere, and useful.

This is not about escaping everyday life. It is an invitation to become more present within it.
